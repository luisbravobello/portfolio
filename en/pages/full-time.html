Local business / Laundry and tailoring

FULL TIME A digital presence that brings a business closer to its customers.

A website showcasing FULL TIME's services and helping people find the business, check its opening hours and get in touch by WhatsApp or phone.

Visit the website (new tab) View repository (new tab)

Type

01 / GOAL

The starting point

People need to know which services are available and how to get in touch without navigating a complicated website. The goal was to make that information visible from the start.

02 / SOLUTION

My approach

I organized the experience around services, trust and contact. Navigation leads to business information and provides clear access to WhatsApp, phone calls and directions.

03 / EXPERIENCE

What you can do

  • Explore laundry, industrial drying and clothing alteration services.
  • Contact the business through WhatsApp, phone and Google Maps.
  • Browse service, garment care, FAQ and contact pages.
  • Use the language selector and mobile navigation.

04 / TAKEAWAY

What I learned

A local business website works better when its content answers specific questions and the next action is within reach.
